Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-09

VURKO Publisher ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy describes what data the App processes.

1. Data We Process

The App accesses your TikTok account (profile info and video publishing) only through TikTok's official APIs, solely to provide video publishing functionality. We do not store your TikTok password.

2. Tokens

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored securely and used only to call TikTok APIs on your behalf. Tokens are never shared with third parties.

3. Content

Videos you publish through the App remain your property. The App does not use your content for any purpose other than executing your publishing requests.

4. Data Retention

We retain API tokens and logs only as long as needed to operate the service. You may revoke access at any time.

5. Contact

For questions, contact: vurko.support@outlook.com
